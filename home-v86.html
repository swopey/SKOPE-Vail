Difficulty is the banner

Four bands, straight off a trail sign: green beginner, blue intermediate, black advanced, platinum black expert. Join Skope takes the same gradient, so the card reads as one object and the rating lands before you read a word.

Both new colours sit deliberately away from the ramp — the green is a deep forest rather than the terrain sage, the blue a true trail blue rather than glacier. That separation matters: the ramp says what kind of thing this is, difficulty says how hard it is, and they'd fight if they shared hues.

Worth noticing why platinum earns the top of the scale. It isn't blacker than black, it's lighter and metallic, the only one of the four with any sheen. Double black is the one rating that should feel like a prize rather than a warning, and going darker would just read as "very advanced."

Dark screen

The band is the same platinum in both themes now, since a rating can't change with the theme. What lifts it off the dark ground is the card body underneath, sitting a couple of steps above the page — so the dark band reads as an object on a lighter card rather than a hole in the screen.

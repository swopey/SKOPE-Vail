What the onboarding writes

Without this the onboarding is a survey: taps happen and nothing is kept. This is the contract between the flow and the planner, and it is what a builder needs in order to wire a screen to anything.

Who wins when two rules disagree

  1. 1. GRAPH LAWS — ORDER-03 and anything owner:derived. Never overridable by a user.
  2. 2. HOURS AND SAFETY — TIME-01, lift closes, the ability ceiling. A preference cannot ski a closed lift.
  3. 3. SHARED RULES — skope-rules.json, owner:hayden.
  4. 4. USER RULES — this file.
  5. 5. THE OPTIMISER — free to choose anything the four layers above leave open.

What it deliberately never asks

Nobody describes their taste in words. Every screen is a pick, because people are bad at describing taste and good at recognising it.
Nobody is asked how many vertical feet they want. That is an output, not an input.
Nobody rates themselves on a slider. USER-01 is a trail symbol and USER-02 is a count of seasons; both are things a person knows about themselves.
Nobody is asked how fast they ski. USER-12 asks about confidence instead, which is the same information without the framing.

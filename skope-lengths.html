SKOPE
Run lengths from OpenStreetMap · Vail · v9
Queries the public Overpass API for every piste:type=downhill way around Vail and adds up its coordinates. Lengths are plan distance, so a steep run measures a few percent short of the real slope distance. Nothing is sent anywhere. Your aliases and your not-on-OSM calls are kept in this browser only.
A run you split by difficulty is split the same way in OSM, because a way can only carry one piste:difficulty — so segments are measured one by one, never given the whole run’s length.
Ready. Press fetch — it takes 10 to 30 seconds.

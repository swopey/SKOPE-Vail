SKOPE
Run lengths from OpenStreetMap · Vail
Queries the public Overpass API for every piste:type=downhill way around Vail and adds up its coordinates. Lengths are plan distance, so a steep run measures a few percent short of the real slope distance. Nothing is sent anywhere and nothing is stored.
Ready. Press fetch — it takes 10 to 30 seconds.
